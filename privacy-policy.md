# Privacy Policy for Stranger Chat

Last updated: June 4, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Stranger Chat ("the App") provides anonymous 1-to-1 chat between users. No registration, login, email address, phone number, or legal identity is required to use the App.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed by the App, how it is used, when it may be shared with service providers, how long it is retained, and how you can contact us about privacy or deletion requests.

## 2. Information We Do Not Require

We do not require:

- Legal name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Contact list
- Uploaded photos
- Precise GPS location
- Payments or financial data
- Social login

The App may use an anonymous app ID, generated or chosen display name, emoji avatar, selected interests, push notification token, device information, approximate location inferred by service providers, and usage or diagnostics data as described below.

We do not send chat message text, direct message content, partner IDs, exact interests, or revealed names to advertising or analytics services.

## 3. Information We Process To Provide The App

The App uses a WebSocket-based communication system to connect random users for real-time chat. The following data may be processed to run the chat experience and related features:

- A locally generated anonymous app/user ID
- Generated or chosen display name and emoji avatar
- Selected interests, if you choose interests before starting a chat
- Messages exchanged during an active stranger chat session
- Direct messages between mutual favorites
- Typing indicators
- Matchmaking status
- Session timestamps
- Connection, disconnection, reconnection, and app interaction events
- Favorites and rematch status
- Push notification token and notification preferences, when notification permission is granted
- Device information such as device model, manufacturer, operating system, app version, and language/locale
- Diagnostics and reliability information such as crashes, errors, performance, and network status
- Device or advertising identifiers used by Firebase, Google AdMob, Expo/FCM, or the operating system
- Approximate location such as country, region, or city inferred from IP address, device signals, or advertising/analytics services

We do not request precise location permission from the device.

### Anonymous Stranger Chat Messages

Anonymous stranger chat messages are transmitted through our server to deliver the real-time chat feature. They are not stored as regular message history. They exist only during the active session and are destroyed when the session ends.

### Direct Messages Between Mutual Favorites

If two users mutually save each other as favorites, they may exchange direct messages. Direct messages are stored on our servers solely to provide the direct messaging feature.

### AI Chat Fallback

If no real partner is available, the App may temporarily connect you with an AI chat partner. Messages sent during an AI fallback conversation may be sent to Google's Gemini API or related Google AI service so the AI partner can generate a response. These messages are used for app functionality and are not sent by us to advertising or analytics services.

## 4. How We Use Data

Data is used to:

- Pair users for anonymous chat
- Deliver messages in real time
- Show typing indicators and connection state
- Manage matchmaking, disconnection, reconnection, favorites, rematches, and direct messages
- Provide temporary AI fallback chat when enabled
- Send push notifications when permission is granted and notifications are enabled
- Understand aggregate app usage and reliability
- Diagnose crashes, errors, abuse, fraud, and service problems
- Display, measure, and protect ads
- Maintain security, prevent misuse, and comply with legal obligations

We do not sell chat content or user identity information.

## 5. Sharing With Service Providers

We share data only as needed to operate, secure, analyze, improve, and monetize the App. Service providers may process data according to their own privacy policies and SDK disclosures.

### Firebase Analytics

The App uses Firebase Analytics to understand aggregate app usage, such as screen views, queue joins, chat pairings, message counts, game usage, referrals, notification permission status, notification opens, and feature usage.

Firebase Analytics may process pseudonymous identifiers, app interactions, device information, diagnostics, approximate location, and analytics events. Analytics events do not include chat message text, direct message content, partner IDs, exact interests, revealed names, contact details, or precise location.

### Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, coarse device information, ad interactions, app interactions, and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, security, and compliance, subject to your device settings and Google policies.

We do not send chat message text, direct message content, partner IDs, exact interests, or revealed names to AdMob.

### Expo Notifications And FCM

The App uses Expo notifications and platform push notification services, including Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android, to send push notifications when notification permission is granted and notifications are enabled. Push services may process push tokens, device identifiers, notification payloads, and delivery diagnostics to deliver notifications.

### Google Gemini / Google AI Services

When AI fallback chat is used, message text and recent conversation context may be sent to Google Gemini or related Google AI services to generate AI responses. This is used only to provide the AI chat feature.

### Fly.io

The App uses a backend server hosted on Fly.io for WebSocket communication, matchmaking, push-token registration, favorites, direct messages, and app functionality.

## 6. Data Retention

Anonymous stranger chat messages are not stored as regular history. They exist in server memory during the active session and are destroyed when the session ends.

AI fallback conversation context is used during the active AI session and is not stored as regular chat history by us.

Direct messages between mutual favorites are retained for up to 30 days from the date they are sent, then permanently deleted. Direct messages are also deleted when either user removes the other from favorites.

Push notification tokens and notification preferences are retained while needed to provide notifications, until they become stale, are replaced, or are deleted.

Favorites, display names, avatars, and related app data are retained while needed to provide App features or until they expire, are removed, or are deleted.

Diagnostics, security, and server logs may be retained for a limited period to operate, secure, debug, and improve the App. Logs are not intended to contain chat message text or legal identity information.

Third-party analytics, advertising, notification, and AI providers may retain data according to their own policies.

## 7. Deletion Requests

The App does not require users to create accounts. You can request deletion of server-side data associated with your anonymous app ID by contacting us at the email address below.

If you request deletion, please include your anonymous app ID if available. We will make reasonable efforts to delete data associated with that ID, such as push tokens, favorites, direct messages, profile/display information, and notification preferences, unless retention is required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or another legitimate reason.

You can also delete direct message history with a favorite by removing that person from your favorites inside the App.

Some data may already have been deleted because anonymous chat messages are not stored as regular history. Data processed by third-party analytics, advertising, notification, or AI providers may need to be managed through device settings or those providers' privacy controls.

## 8. Security

We use encrypted connections in production to protect communication between the App and our backend services.

Anonymous chat messages are not stored as regular stranger chat history. Direct messages between mutual favorites are stored in a secured database and transmitted over encrypted connections.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we use reasonable safeguards to protect the information processed by the App.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Because the App allows anonymous user-generated content, it is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly allow children under 16 to use the App or knowingly collect data from children under 16.

If you believe a child has provided data through the App, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

## 10. User-Generated Content And Safety

Because conversations are anonymous and temporary, you should not share personal details or send harmful, illegal, or inappropriate content. Misuse of the App may lead to access restriction.
